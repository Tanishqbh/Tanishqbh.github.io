 I am a 5th year Mathematics graduate student at Northeastern University working under [Prof. Jose Perea](joperea.com). 

 My thesis work consists of devloping *Toroidal Methods in Machine Learning*. One such method uses Eilenberg--Maclane based [*Toroidal Coordinates*](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/storage/00lipics/lipics-vol258-socg2023/LIPIcs.SoCG.2023.57/LIPIcs.SoCG.2023.57.pdf), to compute a map $$X \to \mathbb{T}^d$$ and produces an inverse $$F:\mathbb{T}^d \to X$$ to extract an explicit parameterization of the underlying torus. A dual method assigns an torus, called the *weighted Jacobian*, to a space that is invariant under a geometric process, such as subdivision.
